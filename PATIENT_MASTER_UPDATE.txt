Patient Master integration update

Implemented:
- Reads patient_master from Supabase using existing supabase-config.js.
- Matches patient_master to Medica patients by MRN only.
- Patient cards show National ID, Mobile, and Short Address.
- Full Address is available through a View Full Address button/modal.
- Specialty remains from Medica and is not overwritten from patient_master.
- Admin includes Patient Master completeness filters:
  Complete
  Missing National ID
  Missing Mobile
  Missing Short Address
  Any Missing Data
  All
- Admin shows total and missing-data counts and searchable rows.

Expected patient_master columns:
mrn, patient_name, national_id, mobile, short_national_address, full_address
